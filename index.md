# Welcome to My Website

I’m currently an assistant professor in Tunghai Univeristy. 

My research focused on racial politics, political behaviors, and international relations. I specialize in examining the voting patterns of Asian Americans and their engagement in the political process. Additionally, I investigate how political elites interact with ethnic minorities. Another area of focus in my research is the study of political participation and elections in Taiwan, particularly online campaigning.

My work has been featured in the scholarly journal <em>New Media & Society, Journal of the East-West Center, and Taiwan Democracy Quarterly</em>. I have also written various non-peer-reviewed pieces discussing minority participation in elections and the electoral landscape in Taiwan and the U.S.

I'm excited and motivated to learn novel research techniques and enhance my coding skills using Python and R.

Welcome to contact me via email.
